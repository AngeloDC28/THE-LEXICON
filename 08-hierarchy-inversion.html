01 · PROMINENCE
The lens is the headline.

The analytical tag occupies the top of the card with equal visual weight to anything below. It is not a decoration; it is the argument.

02 · COLOUR
Tags carry a hue.

Each category family has an accent colour drawn from a controlled palette. Across a grid, the colours reveal which arguments are present — at a glance.

03 · METADATA
Designer becomes context.

The designer, season, and location remain visible — as monospace metadata below the image. They are the where and when, not the what.

04 · INTERACTION
Tags are filters.

Clicking the tag pivots the archive to show every entry under that category — making the editorial taxonomy navigable, not decorative.

The argument, made scannable.

Four cards across the grid; four analytical positions. The colour-coded tags make the editorial landscape legible before the visitor reads a single word of body copy. This is what it means to subvert the codes of fashion media in the structure itself.
