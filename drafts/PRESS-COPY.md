## Note for Angelo — one thing you need to set up

`press@thelexicon.xyz` does not exist yet. A press page listing a Gmail address undercuts the whole exercise — it's the first thing a publicist's eye goes to.

You own the domain, so this is a forwarding rule, not a mailbox: most registrars and Cloudflare Email Routing both do free forwarding to your existing inbox. Ten minutes, one-time, and it makes every email you send for the next two years look like it came from a publication.

Set the forward, then send yourself a test before the page goes live.

## Note on the offer to interview crew

The paragraph inviting costume designers is doing real work — it's the specific thing that distinguishes you from every other new film outlet asking for screeners. Publicists are chronically short of outlets willing to cover below-the-line crew, and craft campaigns need that coverage in awards season. Leave it in.
