## GROUP A — public domain, downloadable, no permission needed

Only two, and they're the images for the strongest-timed review. Do these first.

### `marie-antoinette-01.jpg`
**Élisabeth Louise Vigée Le Brun, *Marie Antoinette in a Chemise Dress*, 1783**

- **Where:** the Fashion History Timeline entry (Fashion Institute of Technology) reproduces it at good resolution with full scholarly apparatus — https://fashionhistory.fitnyc.edu/1783-vigee-le-brun-antoinette-chemise/
- Also on Wikimedia Commons — search `Marie Antoinette chemise Vigée Le Brun`. Take the largest file offered.
- **rights:** `public-domain`
- **credit:** `Élisabeth Louise Vigée Le Brun, Marie Antoinette in a Chemise Dress, 1783. Oil on canvas, 89.8 × 72 cm. Hessische Hausstiftung, Kronberg.`
- **alt:** `Marie Antoinette in a loose white muslin chemise dress and straw hat, holding a rose`
- **caption:** `Withdrawn from the 1783 Salon. The scandal was the muslin.`

> ⚠️ **Provenance note — read before publishing.** This painting exists in several versions, and the fate of the one actually shown at the 1783 Salon is disputed; some sources describe the Salon original as lost. The Kronberg attribution comes from the Fashion History Timeline and is defensible, but credit *the specific version you download* — if the file you take is from a different collection, change the credit line to match. Don't inherit my wording blindly for this one.

### `marie-antoinette-02.jpg`
**Élisabeth Louise Vigée Le Brun, *Marie Antoinette with a Rose*, 1783**

- **Where:** Wikimedia Commons, category `Marie Antoinette with the Rose (Élisabeth Vigée-Lebrun - Versailles)` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Marie_Antoinette_with_the_Rose_(%C3%89lisabeth_Vig%C3%A9e-Lebrun_-_Versailles) — and the file `File:Vigée-Lebrun Marie Antoinette 1783.jpg`. **Check which painting you've got before saving** — the two 1783 portraits are frequently mislabelled as each other. The rose version is the one in blue-grey silk with pearls.
- Also on the Fashion History Timeline — https://fashionhistory.fitnyc.edu/1783-vigee-le-brun-antoinette/
- **rights:** `public-domain`
- **credit:** `Élisabeth Louise Vigée Le Brun, Marie Antoinette with a Rose, 1783. Oil on canvas, 116.8 × 88.9 cm. Château de Versailles.`
- **alt:** `Marie Antoinette in blue-grey silk with pearls, holding a rose`
- **caption:** `The replacement. Silk, formality, and a queen dressed as the Salon required.`

> Worth knowing: this painting currently hangs in the ante-dining room of the **Petit Trianon** — the same building hosting the Coppola exhibition. Consider adding a clause to the review.

These are copyrighted film stills. **Screenshot them from your own copy of each film.** Don't pull them off image-aggregator sites: those copies are frequently mis-credited, and the fair-dealing position is cleanest when the frame comes from a legitimate viewing.

`rights` for every file in this group: **`fair-dealing`**
