How the scale interpolates.

Each clamp() defines a minimum (locked below the small viewport), a preferred fluid value (scales with vw), and a maximum (locked above the large viewport). Plotted across viewports, type sizes follow controlled diagonals — H1 climbs steeply, body climbs gently. Hierarchy is preserved at every width.
