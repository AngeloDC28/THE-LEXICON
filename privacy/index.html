Privacy · updated 17 November 2026

What we store, and why.

You can read everything on THE LEXICON without an account. There is no tracking, no advertising and no analytics.

If you just browse

Nothing about you is sent to us. A few settings are kept in your own browser so the site remembers them: your theme, your language, shows you have bookmarked or recently opened, reading mode, and whether you have seen the cookie notice. They never leave your device. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

If you sign in

  • Your email address. Used only to send you a one-time sign-in link. The link expires after 15 minutes and works once.
  • Your folders. The names of the folders you create, the shows you save into them and any notes you add, so they appear on every device you sign in on.
  • A sign-in cookie. One secure cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is essential for signing in and is not used for anything else.

Sign-in emails are sent through our email provider (Resend). Accounts and folders are stored in our database host (Neon). Both process it only on our behalf.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. You can do that at any time from the sign-in menu (Delete account): your email, your folders and everything in them are deleted at once.

Under 18?

You can use everything on the site without signing in. If you are under 13, ask a parent or carer before you create an account. We never ask for your age, name or location, there are no public profiles, comments or messages, and nothing you save can be seen by anyone else.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you. Email info@thelexicon.xyz. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
