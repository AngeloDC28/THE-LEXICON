Images and copyright

The runway images are editorial and press photographs from publicly available archives, press releases and published fashion media. They appear in direct support of written commentary (the notes, critique and analysis on each page) and are used under the fair dealing exception for criticism and review in sections 30 and 30A of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988.

Copyright in every image stays with its rights holders: photographers, fashion houses and agencies. No commercial use is made of them. They are reproduced at a size suited to analysis, not as a replacement for licensed copies.

Where the photographer and source are known, they are credited under each image. Rights holders who want to be credited, or who object to their work being included, should email info@thelexicon.xyz. Requests are dealt with within 14 days.
